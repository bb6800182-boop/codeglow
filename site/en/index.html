Why I made it

At the checkout you open one app for a loyalty card, another for a coupon, and there is a queue behind you. When you finally find the barcode, the screen is too dim and it takes a few tries to scan.

All I wanted was to show a barcode. So CodeGlow puts it right on the home screen: one tap and it appears at full brightness; tap again and the brightness goes back to what it was.

Three home-screen widgets

Long-press an empty spot on the home screen → “Widgets” → “CodeGlow”, and pick the one that suits your layout.

Single barcode

The barcode sits on the home screen and stretches as wide as you make the widget. Tap the sun for full brightness, tap again to restore.

Several stores

All your store icons in a row. Tap one to show just that barcode at full brightness; tap the barcode to go back.

Single icon

Just one store icon, taking almost no space. Tap it to turn it into the barcode, tap again to turn it back.

Simple, with what you need

Membership cards

Code 128, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, Code 39, ITF, Codabar and QR Code. The format is picked from the number you type, and you can change it yourself.

Full brightness, automatically

The screen goes to full brightness while a barcode is showing and returns to your previous brightness and auto-brightness afterwards. In the app, tapping a barcode shows it full screen and keeps the screen on.

Store icons

Pick an image as the icon for each barcode, or let the app use the first letter of its name.

Taiwan e-invoice carrier

If you shop in Taiwan, the mobile barcode for e-invoices (8 characters starting with “/”) is supported too.

Your data stays on the phone

The app has no network permission. Barcodes are stored only on this phone: nothing is uploaded, and there are no ads or analytics.

Install and set up

  1. Download and install the APKIt is not on Google Play. Open this page on your phone, tap “Download APK”, and allow your browser to install unknown apps when asked.
  2. Add a barcodeOpen CodeGlow, tap “+ Add”, type a name and the number. You will see the barcode right away.
  3. Allow “Modify system settings”Widgets need this permission to brighten the screen. The app shows a prompt; tap “Allow”. Without it the barcode still shows, it just will not get brighter.
  4. Put a widget on the home screenLong-press an empty spot → “Widgets” → “CodeGlow”, drag one to the home screen and choose which barcode it shows.

Questions

Why does it need “Modify system settings”?

A home-screen widget has no window of its own, so the only way to make the screen brighter is to change the system brightness, and Android requires you to switch that permission on yourself. CodeGlow uses it only to raise and restore brightness.

Showing a barcode full screen inside the app does not need the permission.

What if the scanner cannot read it?

First make sure the screen is at full brightness (tap the sun on the single-barcode widget). On a narrow widget the bars get very thin: make the widget wider, or open the app and tap the barcode to show it full screen.

If it still fails, the store may use a different barcode format: open “Edit” and try another one.

Where are my barcodes stored? What about a new phone?

Only on this phone. The app has no network permission and is excluded from system backups.

So after changing phones or removing the app you will need to enter your barcodes again.

Which phones are supported?

Android 12 or later. Developed and tested on a Pixel 7 Pro; widget sizes and long-press menus differ a little between launchers.

Which languages does the app have?

English and Traditional Chinese. It follows your phone’s language: Chinese phones show Chinese, everything else shows English.

I installed a version before 0.4.0. How do I update?

From 0.4.0 the package name changed along with the name CodeGlow, so your phone treats it as a different app and it cannot be installed over the old one.

Install the new version, enter your barcodes and place your widgets again, then remove the old app. Later updates install normally.

A warning appears during installation?

Because the app is not installed from Google Play, Android asks you to allow “install unknown apps”, and Play Protect may ask once more. The source code is public, so you can check it yourself.

Privacy: no network access, no data collected. Your barcodes stay on your phone.
